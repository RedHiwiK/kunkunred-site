App Store pages, a website for each app, repositories on GitHub. After shipping a few apps, my work was scattered across a lot of places. I wanted one place that ties it together, so here it is.

Three kinds of things will live here:

- **Apps**: the three apps on the App Store, each with its own page.
- **Writing**: things worth writing down from indie development, covering product, code, and handing repetitive work to AI.
- **About**: who I am and how I work.

The site itself is static pages generated by Astro, hosted on GitHub Pages. A new post is one Markdown file. Keeping it that easy is the only way I'll keep writing.

If you'd like to follow along, there's an [RSS feed](../../rss.xml).
